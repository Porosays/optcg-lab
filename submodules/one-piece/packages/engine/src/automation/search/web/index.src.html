Cards

“Played” = how often a copy got used. “Win lift” = win rate in games where it was used minus the deck's win rate. “Stuck” = copies left in hand at game end. Low played + high stuck = a cut candidate.

Paste two versions of a deck (say, with and without a tech card). Both play the same opponents with the same shuffles, so the difference comes from your changes and not from luck.

A difference under about 5 points with a few hundred games is usually noise. Run more games before trusting a small edge.

How it works

The full One Piece TCG rules engine (from the open-source tcg-engines project) runs inside your browser in background workers (up to four for tests, two for custom batches). Nothing is sent to a server. Each game shuffles both decks, picks who goes first at random, and has two bots play it out under the real rules.

Fields are real lists: current EB-05 meta, OP-18 lists from X and LogDuel, OP-16 era decks, and classic 2023–24 decks for Extra Regulation.

Known limits (be skeptical of close numbers)

  • The bots now hold DON!! for leaders that turn it into defense (like OP13-001 Luffy), but they still rarely save DON!! for [Counter] Events, so counter-event-heavy decks are a bit under-rated.
  • 11 cards still have no coded effect (mostly OP-18 cards with unusual text). The OP-18 Karoo and Ms. All Sunday leaders are in.
  • The sim matched real-world matchup reports on 7 of 20 checked matchups before the latest fixes. Treat it as a research tool, not a tier list.
