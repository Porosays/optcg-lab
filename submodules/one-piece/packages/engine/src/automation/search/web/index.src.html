How it works

The full One Piece TCG rules engine (from the open-source tcg-engines project) runs inside your browser in background workers, one per CPU core. Nothing is sent to a server. Each game shuffles both decks, picks who goes first at random, and has two bots play it out under the real rules.

Fields are real lists: current EB-05 meta, OP-18 lists from X and LogDuel, OP-16 era decks, and classic 2023–24 decks for Extra Regulation.

Known limits (be skeptical of close numbers)

  • The bots rarely hold DON!! back, so counter events and "keep DON up" leaders (like OP13-001 Luffy) are under-rated.
  • 23 cards still have no coded effect, including the OP-18 Karoo and Ms. All Sunday leaders.
  • Some OP-17 Sabo and Xebec games stall and count as no result.
  • So far the sim matches real-world matchup reports on 7 of 20 checked matchups. Treat it as a research tool, not a tier list.
